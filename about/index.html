About

Welcome to Blind POV.

Thanks for dropping in. Here's a bit about me.

My name is Greg Osborne. I live in Chillicothe, Ohio. I had full vision until I was thirteen. Since then, I've been totally blind — I don't see colors, shapes, light, or dark. I've been completely blind for over thirty-five years.

I have a degree in computer science. As you can imagine, I've done a lot with computers, and I continue to do so. I love Linux, iPhones, music, guitars, drums, audio-described TV and movies, leatherworking, and lots more. I am a suicide attempt survivor.
