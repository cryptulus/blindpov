Introduction to Blind Point of View

Published: November 13, 2026

Welcome to blind POV. My name is Greg Osborne, and this blog is one totally blind person's Point of view regarding accessibility and technology in the world today.

To understand where I am coming from, let me tell you a bit about myself. I had full vision until age 13. After that, I lost all sight. I have a degree in computer science, and I have now had over 35 years experience with complete vision loss. I have seen a lot of accessibility trends come and go. This blog will be my attempt to show a blind person's perspective regarding some of the current accessibility and technology trends.
